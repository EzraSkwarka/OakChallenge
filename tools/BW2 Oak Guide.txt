1. The Basics


   1. What is a Professor Oak Challenge?


Professor Oak has always had a dream to fill the pokedex, but he’s too old to do it! It’s up to you as a new trainer to complete that pokedex as much as you can independently by catching, evolving and breeding as many pokemon as is possible. Professor Oak also isn’t getting any younger, so you need to complete that dex as a priority! Maybe you should put your gym challenge on hold for a while…


In 2018, a Reddit user named Chamale posted updates of a unique challenge on Pokemon Crystal which at the time didn’t have a name but was referred to as ‘Oak Mode’ or a ‘Professor Oak Run/Challenge’. I was really intrigued by this style of gameplay and set out to play this on FireRed, writing a guide as I went along. After the success of the run I tried it on different versions and have now played the majority of the main series games and even a couple of spin offs. 


The basic concept of the challenge follows these simple rules:
* You have to catch and evolve (or breed) as many pokemon as is possible in the game until you can’t proceed any further until a gym badge is required e.g. needing a badge to be able to use a HM move or in most cases, to allow you to proceed with the plot or to get NPCs to move out of the way. For example, you will need to fully evolve your starter amongst others before defeating the Aspertia Gym for your first badge. For parts where gyms can be done in any order, always get the badge that unlocks the most pokemon.
* You cannot trade with other versions. This means that trade evolutions are off the table, you’ll get no version exclusives from the other version and you’re stuck with the decisions you make such as your starter. In-game trades are allowed as that NPC is in your version of the game.
* (Optional) – Call yourself ‘Oak’ or ‘Prof. Oak’ for fun.


   2. Tips on this version


* Grinding – The first section of a Professor Oak Challenge is always very daunting as you need to get your starter fully evolved. In Generation 5, experience gains have changed a lot. The amount of exp isn’t determined solely by the level and species of the pokemon you’ve defeated. It now takes your level into consideration. The higher your level is compared to the opponent means that exp is reduced. This gets really awkward when you get past level 20 in the first section of the run and your exp per battle starts dwindling. By the time your starter is about to evolve you will literally be earning 11-12 exp per battle against the highest levelled (Lv. 7) wild pokemon! There is also no way to rematch trainers in Black 2 and White 2 as there is no Vs Seeker. Audino is your best friend in this challenge. Once you have your first badge you can start finding them in the rustling grass and they give HUGE exp. 


* Learning new moves shouldn’t be taken for granted, particularly with pokemon that evolve at higher levels. During a normal game you might be quick to replace a weak move with a stronger one but getting rid of non-attacking moves is a better strategy here. On the first part of the challenge before the Aspertia Gym, you are battling low level pokemon and will run out of PP very quickly while that exp bar goes up very slowly. You need to maximise the amount of pokemon you can defeat, so having multiple attacking moves, regardless of how weak they are, will be beneficial. You have no need for moves like Growl or Sand Attack. Also consider keeping three stage pokemon unevolved so that they learn moves faster than their middle stage would.


* This challenge is a fun alternative to a regular play through and you may really appreciate having to train pokemon up yourself that you never trained before. On the other hand you may end up despising certain pokemon due to their inability to attack when you first get them or their generally poor stats/levelling up moves. 


* Please note that this is not a full guide to the game, I am only listing the pokemon you can catch at the earliest opportunities and any required items. Please consult other guides for a full walkthrough. This guide assumes you know how to get through the caves etc throughout the game.


* And finally, remember that this is YOUR challenge. There are things you can do to make this run easier or harder and it’s entirely at your discretion how you want to handle this. A lot of people like to use pokemon with the Pickup ability (e.g. Lillipup) to farm Rare Candies to level up your pokemon. You could use exp share, you could put pokemon in the day-care and you could switch train pokemon (send it out and then replace it with a better pokemon). With rustling grass/dust clouds, aside from Audino there is usually a pokemon on each route that can be encountered at a lower rate, often this is a fully evolved pokemon. It’s up to you if you just catch them fully evolved or train them up and evolve their earlier stage.

This first part will cover everything you can do from the get-go right up to the Aspertia City Gym BEFORE you get that first badge from Cheren. This WILL take a while so it will really test your patience…


Aspertia City - 
In a rare occurrence, not only do you start off in a city rather than a town but you’re also in the location of the first gym! Not that you can access it yet, especially seeing how there’s lots of Pokémon to catch. As soon as the game begins you’ll need to see Bianca who is up the steps at the northern end of town. There’s no real advantage to any of the starters as they all evolve at level 36. There’s also no real type advantage against the wild pokemon, eventually you’ll one shot everything.


* Snivy --(Lv. 17)-->Servine--(Lv. 36)-->Serperior
OR
* Tepig--(Lv. 17)-->Pignite--(Lv. 36)-->Emboar
OR
* Oshawott--(Lv. 17)-->Dewott--(Lv. 36)-->Samurott


Route 19– 
Leave your home town via the northern exit and you’ll get the usual capture tutorial. There’s only two pokemon to catch here - Patrat and Purrloin. Head east towards Floccesy Town and you’ll meet the former Unova Champion, Alder.


* Patrat--(Lv. 20)-->Watchog
* Purrloin--(Lv. 20)-->Liepard


Route 20 – 
Follow Alder’s instructions and leave out of the east side of town to reach Route 20. Sewaddle, Pidove and Sunkern live here, with the latter two swapping encounter rates depending on the season. Pidove is usually common and Sunkern is rare, except for Summer when Sunkern is more abundant. To get that Sun Stone for Sunkern you’ll need a Lv31 or above Pickup Pokémon (Lillipup) in order to get a chance to hold one. 


Once Sewaddle evolves into Swadloon it will need a sufficient level of happiness/friendship to evolve. Try not to let it faint as this will lower friendship. Levelling up also boosts friendship but with the exp curve you don’t want to level up anything further than you need to. Keeping it in your party while you run around grinding other pokemon is always the best option. Get its exp bar as close as you can to levelling up and then periodically save your game and see if it gets the evolution once you do hit that level up. If you don’t, soft reset and try again later. 


* Sunkern--(Sun Stone)-->Sunflora
* Pidove--(Lv. 21)-->Tranquill--(Lv. 32)-->Unfezant
* Sewaddle--(Lv. 20)-->Swadloon--(Happiness)-->Leavanny


Floccesy Ranch – 
Your rival, Hugh, will be here trying to find a lost Herdier. The ranch owners will be glad that you’re also here to help and will heal your pokemon whenever you talk to them. With this route having the highest levelled Pokémon of the section, this makes for a great training ground. Here you’ll find Lillipup, Mareep, Psyduck, Riolu and Azurill. Those last two are also happiness based evolutions so try to keep them in your party too. Marill evolves at Lv18 so definitely make sure Azurill evolves at Lv17 at the latest. Riolu also needs to specifically be levelled up during the day so keep that in mind. Once you’ve rescued Herdier you can return to Alder, battle a couple of trainers and can then access the Aspertia Gym.


Lillipup has two abilities which can come in handy. Vital Spirit means that, if it’s in the lead spot (even if KO’d), higher level pokemon are more likely to appear. The other ability is Pickup, which you will need for an evolution stone in this section or you can use it (along with other Lillipup) in this section to get Rare Candies if it’s Lv21+. It loses both of these abilities when it evolves, so consider carefully when to do that (if at all). Given that you’ll need a Lv31 Pickup Pokémon in this section, make that the one that you evolve up to Stoutland once you have all the Pickup items you need. 


* Lillipup--(Lv. 16)-->Herdier--(Lv. 32)-->Stoutland
* Azurill--(Happiness)-->Marill--(Lv. 18)-->Azumarill
* Psyduck--(Lv. 33)-->Golduck
* Riolu--(Happiness during the day)-->Lucario
* Mareep--(Lv. 15)-->Flaaffy--(Lv. 30)-->Ampharos


And with that we’ve hit the wall already! We need to fully evolve everything before we tackle the gym leader. 


Levelling up tips – 
As mentioned already, the exp gain is horrible to you in this game. In comparison to the original Black and White games, they only had four Pokémon to evolve in the first section which makes this game by far one of the longest first sections of a POC. Training a pokemon up to Level 36 is a mammoth task. If your rules allow it, train a Pickup squad (5 Lillipup with Pickup) but the downside to this is that they need to be at least Lv21 to get Rare Candies, which means you’ll have to put in a lot of effort just to assemble the squad in the first place. There are also very limited areas you can train in. Literally, Floccesy Ranch is your best spot. I kept my pokemon unevolved as long as possible so they’d get more attacking moves ASAP to be able to defeat more pokemon without healing. Whilst you’re grinding, consider putting a KO’d Vital Spirit Lillipup in the lead slot so that you’re more likely to get those level 7 pokemon. As great as Floccesy Ranch is for the free healing, that means you’ll either need to faint your Vital Spirit Lillipup every time or you’ll need to run back to the Pokémon Center and deposit/withdraw the rest of your team to heal them. Try to delay battling the gym trainers until your Pokémon are at high levels so you can take advantage of the additional exp when it’s most needed.

New Encounter Methods - 
Going back towards Route 20 with your first badge in hand you’ll be caught up to by Bianca who will give you the new Habitat Mode for your pokedex as well as advising you of rustling spots that can be found all over the region. These are key to earning exp really quickly as well as finding some rare and often fully evolved pokemon. Back at either Floccesy Ranch or Route 20 you’ll be able to encounter Dunsparce if you’re lucky as well as the magnificent Audino. This thing is literally a walking sack of experience points and is your new best friend for the remainder of the challenge. Running up and down to get the grass to shake is pretty much all you’ll ever need. 


* Audino
* Dunsparce


You’re now able to pass the Hiker on Route 20 and as you progress slightly farther into the route you’ll also meet up with Cheren who explains the dark grass on the route which typically contain higher levelled pokemon and the occasional double battle will trigger as you run around in this grass. This is exactly what you should do to get Venipede.  


* Venipede--(Lv. 22)-->Whirlipede--(Lv. 30)-->Scolipede


Virbank City - 
As you enter you’ll see the gym leader Roxie who returns to her gym after an argument with her father. There’s not much to do in town at this point but you can enter the Virbank Complex at the south western part of town. It is split into two areas. The entrance area with the blue/purple-roofed buildings and the main area further south which has many pipes, boilers, furnaces etc. There’s a few trainers around but obviously you’re here for new pokemon. All of the pokemon you need can be found in the main area which includes Magnemite, Koffing, Growlithe and our first version exclusives - Magby in Black 2 or Elekid in White 2. Aside from Koffing, none of these pokemon can fully evolve yet due to the no trading rule, no way to get Fire Stones yet and no access to the location required to evolve Magneton. And with that we’ve reached the next wall…quite a short section in comparison to the last one. 


* Magnemite--(Lv. 30)-->Magneton
* Koffing--(Lv. 35)-->Weezing
* Growlithe
* (B2) Magby--(Lv. 30)-->Magmar
* (W2) Elekid--(Lv. 30)-->Electabuzz


Levelling up tips – 
Audino. And that’s pretty much the answer to every section after this too. There’s no level difference between the Audino in the two Virbank Complex areas. They’ll level between 10-13 and have a 100% encounter rate in the rustling grass. I chose to stick to the entrance area because there’s too much machinery and pipes in the way in the main area and it was much easier to get to rustling spots without triggering a random encounter on the way to the spot. You’ll level up insanely fast at first but once the level gap starts widening you’ll slow down around Lv25+ so there will be either a lot of running around for grass to shake or reliance on your Pickup squad.

Sailing to Castelia - 
As soon as you have your second badge you’ll trigger a trip to PokeStar Studios where you’ll have to do a mandatory tutorial. Roxie’s father, the Captain, heads back to sailing the boat which is great news for us. After you’ve shot a movie you’re free to leave. In the south eastern part of Virbank City you’ll fight a Team Plasma Grunt and will need to head back to Route 20 where you’ll battle another one. Then go back to where you fought the first one and enter the building to speak to the Captain for an instant trip to the enormous Castelia City. 


There are some things around the city that are useful to the challenge including the bike that you’ll be given immediately. The Exp Share can be found by entering the Battle Company. If a pokemon holds this they will receive 50% of the exp even if they didn’t participate. On the very northern street which goes up to Route 4 you can enter a building on the right and be given an Eviolite. This boosts defenses of pokemon that can evolve which is helpful when battling Audino to tank hits better. On the far western street, a lady in one of the buildings will massage pokemon for you once per day which helps with happiness/friendship evolutions. 


Although we don’t want to battle the gym leader yet, speak to the man outside the gym on the western street to trigger a conversation with Iris who heads to the pokemon centre. Follow her and you’ll unlock the Castelia Sewers. 


Castelia Sewers - 
In this cave-like area you’ll team up with your rival and all wild battles will be double battles. Your team will also be healed after every battle so really take advantage of the grinding opportunity. The sewers are home to multiple new pokemon including Zubat, Grimer and Rattata. The collaboration with your rival will end when you confront the two Plasma Grunts which are actually very close to the entrance so you may want to explore the southern part of the sewers for some trainer battles and items. 


* Zubat--(Lv. 22)-->Golbat--(Happiness)-->Crobat
* Grimer--(Lv. 38)-->Muk
* Rattata--(Lv. 20)-->Raticate


Once you’ve defeated the grunts, Burgh will return to the gym and you’re free to enter the back room which is Relic Passage. There are five new pokemon in this room. Timburr, Woobat, Onix and Roggenrola are standard encounters but Drilbur can only be found in a dust cloud. In order to be able to run around enough for this to appear you may need to use a Repel to avoid random encounters. Unlike every cave in the original Black/White games, this cave does NOT spawn evolution stones from dust clouds. 


* Timburr--(Lv. 25)-->Gurdurr
* Woobat--(Happiness)-->Swoobat
* Onix
* Roggenrola--(Lv. 25)-->Boldore
* Drilbur--(Lv. 31)-->Excadrill


Interestingly there’s also another exit out of the sewers which is up a staircase just south of Relic Passage’s entrance. This leads up to a secret park within Castelia City where you’ll be able to run around in the grass to catch Eevee and a couple of version exclusives. Black 2 players will find Buneary and Cottonee while White 2 players will instead find Skitty and Petilil. The shaking grass has a 5% chance of finding the evolved forms of these two version exclusives. If you’re evolving Skitty in White 2 you’ll need a Lv41+ Pickup pokemon in order to find a Moon Stone. You’ll need to catch two Eevee to evolve them into Espeon and Umbreon. The remaining Eeveelutions are not able to be evolved into yet. Catch a second Petilil/Lilligant for a trade coming up. 


* Eevee--(Happiness during the day)-->Espeon
* Eevee--(Happiness during the night)-->Umbreon
* (B2) Buneary--(Happiness)-->Lopunny
* (B2) Cottonee--(Sun Stone)-->Whimsicott
* (W2) Skitty--(Moon Stone)-->Delcatty
* (W2) Petilil--(Sun Stone)-->Lilligant


Route 4 - 
Although we don’t have the badge yet, we can venture a little ways out onto Route 4 which is enough for us to dive into the dark sand to encounter our last few captures - Sandile, Darumaka, Scraggy and either Trubbish in Black 2 or Minccino in White 2. Since we can’t get stones to evolve Minccino, Black 2 edges slightly ahead as Trubbish only needs to evolve by level up. 


* Sandile--(Lv. 29)-->Krokorok--(Lv. 40)-->Krookodile
* Darumaka--(Lv. 35)-->Darmanitan
* Scraggy--(Lv. 39)-->Scrafty
* (B2) Trubbish--(Lv. 36)-->Garbodor
* (W2) Minccino
Levelling up tips – 
Yep, Audino again. You have a few pokemon around Lv40 for evolutions so even against the highest Lv18 Audino it’s still a bit of a grind so consider using your Pickup squad for Rare Candy to finish off the last few levels. If you’re playing White 2, definitely go for the Delcatty rustling grass encounter to avoid excessive levelling up for Lillipup in order to find a Moon Stone via Pickup. For the happiness/friendship evolutions, just keep them in your party while you grind other Pokémon. The amount of biking you’ll do to find Audino will increase your team’s happiness very quickly.

Quite a lengthy section in terms of pokemon caught and ground covered. You’ll be able to venture further into Route 4 now you’ve got the third badge of the region. There is a static encounter that appears on certain days of the week. In Black 2 you’ll find Mandibuzz in this new area with the ruined buildings on Thursdays whereas if you’re playing White 2 then you’ll find Braviary instead on a Monday. Also bring your spare Petilil or Cottonee to find an NPC who will trade the other pokemon for it. Using a Sun Stone from Pickup you can evolve it or you’ll get a free stone coming up. 


* (B2) Mandibuzz
* (W2) Braviary
* (B2) Petilil--(Sun Stone)-->Lilligant
* (W2) Cottonee--(Sun Stone)-->Whimsicott


The Desert Resort - 
On the north western side of Route 4 you’ll find the entrance to the Desert Resort. Pass through the gate into the area proper and battle the nurse for free healing afterwards while you track down all the pokemon in this area. Running in the darker sand you’ll encounter Sandshrew, Dwebble, Trapinch, Sigilyph and Maractus. While you’re in the area, check the very south western part of the route with the dowsing machine that Bianca gave you earlier to find a hidden Fire Stone. Entering the Relic Castle at the north of the route you’ll find Yamask but there’s not much else to do here just yet. 


* Sandshrew--(Lv. 22)-->Sandslash
* Trapinch--(Lv. 35)-->Vibrava--(Lv. 45)-->Flygon
* Dwebble--(Lv. 34)-->Crustle
* Sigilyph
* Maractus
* Yamask--(Lv. 34)-->Cofagrigus


Once you’re done, go back to Route 4 and leave through the north eastern gate to enter Join Avenue. Although it’s technically possible to level this place up to get evolution stones there’s a more straightforward place to get them shortly so carry on through to Nimbasa City. If you are interested in investing some time in Join Avenue you can establish barbers and cafes which give multiple friendship boosts per day if you pay for them which can really speed up happiness/friendship evolutions.  


Nimbasa City - 
Home of the next gym but there’s a lot to do before considering that. Behind the sign to the left of the Pokemon Center is another hidden evolution stone, this time being a Thunder Stone. You’ll need it for this section. In the building to the north west of the Pokemon Center you can speak to an NPC for a free Soothe Bell. For your happiness evolutions, give it this item to hold and actions that would boost a pokemon’s happiness/friendship such as levelling up will have a boosted effect. North of the pokemon center in a building you’ll get a free Sun Stone which you can use for your traded grass type from Route 4 if you didn’t have a spare stone. At the amusement park you may want to pick up the Dropped Item which is vital for some extra pokemon in the post game and you can make a start on this side mission throughout your main game if you want to (see here for more details). 


Lostlorn Forest - 
Making a detour first, head out of the north eastern gate. This will take you to Route 16 which eventually leads to a dead end until post game but you can find the entrance to Lostlorn Forest here. The pokemon on Route 16 are the same as on Route 5 so we’ll skip these for now since there’s other things to do on Route 5. On the western side of the forest you can use the dowsing machine to find a Leaf Stone. In the normal grass encounters you’ll be able to catch Combee, Roselia and either Heracross in Black 2 or Pinsir in White 2. In the rustling grass you’ve got the option of catching Vespiquen to avoid finding a female Combee but you’ll also find Pansage, Pansear, Panpour, Emolga and Roserade. Shiny Stones are not easily obtainable in this section so catching wild Roserade helps out a lot.


Next we come on to the most frustrating part of probably the entire run - Hidden Grottoes. in the north eastern part of the forest you’ll see a dark patch between two trees. Examine this and you’ll be able to enter where you can find either a wild pokemon, an item (which includes evolution stones), an invisible item (use the dowsing machine or check the central patch in the grotto) or nothing at all. Every 256 steps has a small chance of resetting the grotto so you will be biking back and forth a lot for the grotto to refresh and will then need to check to see if there’s anything in it. This is the only way for you to find the other beetle pokemon - Pinsir in Black 2 or Heracross in White 2. If the grotto spawns a pokemon which only happens 20% of the time, the bug pokemon you’ll be looking for only has a 3.75% chance out of the pokemon in the encounter pool. This can easily be the last thing you’ll need to do this section but keep checking back, especially while grinding for Audino in the forest. It’s not the most ideal place to grind because there’s so many pokemon other than Audino in the rustling grass but you’ll be close by to keep checking that grotto. 


* Combee
* Vespiquen
* Roselia
* Roserade
* Pansage
* Pansear
* Panpour
* Emolga
* Pinsir
* Heracross


Route 5 - 
This time going out of the north western exit from Nimbasa City you’ll reach Route 5 which shares its grass encounters with Route 16. You’ll find your version exclusive Psychic type - Gothita for Black 2 or Solosis for White 2. Black 2 players will also now be able to catch a Minccino as well as its evolution Cinccino in rustling grass, something that White 2 players will also want to catch due to the lack of Shiny Stones in the previous section. White 2 players get to catch and evolve Trubbish. There’s another hidden grotto here and Bianca will give you a quick overview. In this grotto you can catch a Foongus which, true to form, will disguise itself as an item so be sure to check it. Thankfully it’s a 75% chance if the grotto refresh contains a pokemon so it’s a lot more bearable. 


* (B2) Gothita--(Lv. 32)-->Gothorita--(Lv. 41)-->Gothitelle
* (W2) Solosis--(Lv. 32)-->Duosion--(Lv. 41)-->Reuniclus
* (B2) Minccino
* Cinccino
* (W2) Trubbish--(Lv. 36)-->Garbodor
* Foongus--(Lv. 39)-->Amoonguss


Evolution Stones - 
So there’s a number of pokemon to evolve through stones here and the reason I've left this until last is because you may have picked up some evolution stones whilst checking the hidden grottoes. We have Growlithe to evolve, three Eeveelutions to get and the three elemental monkeys from Lostlorn Forest. We got a free Leaf Stone, Thunder Stone and Fire Stone in this section already so that leaves at a minimum two more Fire Stones and two more Water Stones. The best way to get these is in the Battle Subway. I chose to enter the singles category since you don’t have a lot of tools to make a competitive team so i used three pokemon (my choices were Darmanitan, Flygon and Reuniclus) and you’ll be required to win 7 consecutive battles which will give you 3BP - just enough to buy one stone. You can continue your streak but the second group of 7 trainers only gives you 3BP, you’d have to win 21 battles to get anything substantial but because the battles get harder you may find it worthwhile to simply end your streak and start over. With all the stones it’s possible to evolve the following pokemon:


* Growlithe--(Fire Stone)-->Arcanine
* Eevee--(Water Stone)-->Vaporeon
* Eevee--(Thunder Stone)-->Jolteon
* Eevee--(Fire Stone)-->Flareon
* Pansage--(Leaf Stone)-->Simisage
* Pansear--(Fire Stone)-->Simisear
* Panpour--(Water Stone)-->Simipour


Levelling up tips – 
Audino of course but the location depends on what you’re working on at the time. Lostlorn Forest is where you’ll want to focus on staying until you’ve found the hidden grotto pokemon but Audino’s appearance is a lot lower whereas Route 5 is the better place to train. Biking next to the grotto on that route is a good spot since the rest of the grass is obscured by trees. With Audino capping at Lv24 it’s actually a fairly rough grind with several late 30 to mid 40 evolutions so the Pickup squad may want to make a comeback to take the edge off.

Part 5 - Pre Badge #5 (Driftveil Gym)

Driftveil Drawbridge - 
After getting the badge, take out a couple of Plasma Grunts and Charles the Heartbreaker and you’ll gain access to the bridge where you can run under the shadows that randomly appear to encounter a Ducklett. 


* Ducklett--(Lv. 35)-->Swanna


Driftveil City - 
There’s no Cold Storage area in these games, it’s been replaced by the Pokemon World Tournament (PWT) which you also can’t access just yet. This means your visit to town will be fairly limited. In the north end of town near the gym there’s a house full of ex-Team Plasma members. Sage Rood will give you a Zorua after battling him. On the upper floor of the Pokemon Center you’ll be given some Luxury Balls by speaking to the girl up there. These will come in handy for a friendship evolution in this section. The Dreamyard - 
Striaton Gym is also not operating anymore as a gym so there’s not much to do in town. Head east to the Dreamyard. Latios in Black 2 or Latias in White 2 will make an appearance. If you chase it around the dreamyard it will eventually go to the upper platforms. Save the game after you see it fly around once you’ve gone to the upper platforms as you’ll be thrust into battle with it next time it appears without a chance to back out. In the grass you’ll encounter Munna and Jigglypuff with both evolutions available in shaking grass if you’re short on Moon Stones.

Routes 2 and 1 - 
South from Striaton and you’re on Route 2. In the grass you can encounter Lickitung with Lickilicky being a rare rustling grass encounter (but again it’s easier to evolve Lickitung by using the move reminder). Another hidden grotto which could have Granbull or either of the two Nidoran (both of which can be bred to get the other). 


* Lickitung--(Level up knowing Rollout)-->Lickilicky

Accumula Town is a short stop with only a single important NPC to the run. In the lower part of town someone wants an Excadrill and they will trade you an Ambipom in exchange. You can trade with the same person AGAIN after you battle them, this time giving them a Hippowdon for an Alakazam. Hippowdon is one of the daily swarm pokemon (see further below). 


* Ambipom--(Breed)-->Aipom
* Alakazam--(Breed)-->Abra--(Lv. 16)-->Kadabra


Route 1 has nothing of interest at the moment so continue down the path to reach Nuvema Town and stop by Professor Juniper’s lab. Speaking to Cedric Juniper again will get you the incredibly useful Super Rod which will net plenty of non native pokemon throughout the region. 


Routes 17 and 18 - 
Before we go on a Unovan fishing tour, let’s pay a visit to one of the last new areas of the post game. Head back to Route 1 but do take the time to fish here. You can hook Feebas at a 5% encounter rate or it’s much more common in rippling spots which is also the only way to encounter Milotic which also has a 5% encounter rate. Go west across the water to Route 17, a place I'm sure IGN would love as it’s almost entirely water. Fishing here you can find Horsea and Finneon, with rippling spots including their evolutions (including Kingdra). 


* Feebas
* Milotic
* Horsea--(Lv. 32)-->Seadra
* Kingdra
* Finneon--(Lv. 31)-->Lumineon


Continuing west you’ll hit Route 18 where Carnivine and Tropius lurk in the grass on the island. There’s a hidden grotto here which has a single pokemon that is absolutely necessary - Chatot. Other encounters are available (Kingler and Dragonite) but we’ll be fishing for them very shortly. Make sure to grab the Magma Stone located on this route. Leave the north eastern side of the route to circle back to Route 17 where you’ll be able to reach the P2 Laboratory. Fishing in the rippling water could net you a Qwilfish. 


* Carnivine

Route 15 and Black City/White Forest - 
A very quick detour first for Black 2 players (optional for White 2), go to Floccesy Town and you can challenge Alder. After that, from Nimbasa City, head northeast and you’ll eventually be able to reach Marvelous Bridge. Bringing the Lunar Wing we got from Strange House will let us encounter Cresselia. Save your game early to avoid messing up the encounter. Also make use of the Magikarp salesman as you’ll be able to buy Magikarp which is completely unavailable in Unova otherwise. Onto Route 15 you can find Pupitar in the grass (as well as Tyranitar in shaking grass) and there’s an in-game trade where you can give a Ditto for a Rotom. 


* Cresselia
* Magikarp--(Lv. 20)-->Gyarados
* Pupitar--(Lv. 55)-->Tyranitar--(Breed)-->Larvitar

On the other side of the route you’ll reach Black City or White Forest depending on the version you’re playing. By challenging the battle facility here (only available after defeating Alder) you can challenge and defeat Benga if you advance far enough. Black 2 players will receive a gift from Alder back in Floccesy Town for doing so - a shiny Gible. Not only is it great to get a shiny but you’ll also get three additional dex entries for evolving it and a THIRD Exp Share! White 2 players get a shiny Dratini. Unfortunately Dratini is available through other methods so White 2 will fall short in the final dex count. 


* (B2) Gible--(Lv. 24)-->Gabite--(Lv. 48)-->Garchomp


Legendary Mop-up - 
Taking the Magma Stone from Route 18, go back to Reversal Mountain and you’ll be able to encounter Heatran. As always, save before the encounter just in case. Then go to the Cave of Being which you can Surf to from Route 20. The three lake spirits of Sinnoh will be disturbed and will flee to separate static encounters. Go to the top of Celestial Tower for Mesprit, Route 23 for Azelf and Nacrene City for Uxie. Next go to Clay Tunnel accessed from the north of Driftveil City. You will be able to reach the Underground Ruins where Regirock lurks. Save your game and encounter the titan. Your gift will be a key - the Iron Key for Black 2 players or the Iceberg Key in White 2. If you save the game and soft reset to the start menu you can access Unova Link and use the key to put the corresponding titan in Regirock’s place at the ruins. This means that Black 2 players can catch Registeel and White 2 players can catch Regice. 


* Heatran
* Mesprit
* Azelf
* Uxie
* Regirock
* (B2) Registeel
* (W2) Regice


Funfest Missions - 
Something that I haven’t touched on at all until now, where it is actually mandatory in order to catch some pokemon. If you’ve never been to the Entree Forest/Entralink then switch on your C-Gear on the bottom screen, select Wireless and then Entralink, This will take you to the Entree Forest where an old man will put you through a basic tutorial. After completing it you can return and select any mission you want. If you’re playing Black 2 then you’ll want to select Forgotten Lost Items or in White 2 select Not-Found Lost Items. Both of these will have been unlocked after becoming Champion. Run around various locations investigating the sparkly clouds that appear until you pick up a Plume (White 2)  or Cover Fossil (Black 2). Then simply end the mission, go back to Nacrene City and ask Lenora to give you the other of the two fossils. Reviving these will get you both Unovan fossil pokemon - Archen and Tirtouga. 


Next you’ll want to select the mission Search Hidden Grottoes (also unlocked by becoming Champion) which will require you to find at least one hidden grotto with a pokemon in it. Any grotto is fine so if you’re still hunting for the ones in the post game then give those a look first. By successfully completing this mission you will unlock either Noisy Hidden Grottoes in Black 2 or Quiet Hidden Grottoes in White 2. By activating this mission, some of the hidden grottoes in Unova will have either a Stunky in Black 2 or Glameow in White 2 replaced as its contents which is the only way to catch these pokemon. 


* Archen--(Lv. 37)-->Archeops
* Tirtouga--(Lv. 37)-->Carracosta
* (B2) Stunky--(Lv. 34)-->Skuntank
* (W2) Glameow--(Lv. 38)-->Purugly
Daily Swarms/Outbreaks - 
Something you’ll want to start pinning down as early as possible. Venture into any gate and check the electronic bulletin board. It will tell you of a pokemon outbreak in a certain area of Unova. Head over there to find a decent chance of encountering a pokemon that’s not native to Unova. The game is extremely generous by making the outbreak selections random. If you save the game near a gate, when you switch the game back on the next day you can enter the gate to check the outbreak. If it’s a repeat, then simply soft reset and enter the gate until you’ve caught everything. If you’re not against changing the clock, you can change the time to 23:59 and wait for the clock to roll over to trigger the next daily swarm. The following pokemon can be obtained

Yancy/Curtis Trades -

The trades are on a fixed rotation and they will trade ANY pokemon in exchange so simply hatch or catch pokemon you don’t mind parting with. There’s also a Sinnoh fossil after Ralts but as it’s available already I've excluded it from the list although it’s past its evolution level making it easier to evolve.

Levelling Up Tips - 
I would honestly say to just stick to Audino in the post game areas which you can give your pokemon Exp Share for, switch train with Lucky Egg attached etc. Alternatively you can face the Elite Four over and over with the Exp Share but you actually won’t need to do nearly as much grinding as you’d think looking at the list of available pokemon. Most pokemon are already above their evolutionary levels, the fossils will be the only awkward evolutions to complete although they’re all Rock type and can resist Audino’s attacks.
