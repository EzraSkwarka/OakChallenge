Rare Candy Planner

The Rare Candy Planner helps determine the most efficient use of your available Rare Candies during a Professor Oak Challenge.

Add Pokémon families that still need to be completed, then optionally enter their current levels. The planner works backwards from each family's final evolution level and allocates Rare Candies to the levels that would save the most experience.

  • Add every Pokémon family you still need to evolve.
  • Enter a current level if known, or leave the default value.
  • Set the number of Rare Candies you currently own.
  • Review the planner's recommendations on each card.
